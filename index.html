Why Choose KeiYomi?

Multi-Format Support

Seamlessly read PDF, EPUB, CBZ, CBR, ZIP, TXT, MD, and DOCX files. Perfect for manga, comics, novels, notes, and office documents.

Flexible Reading Modes

Switch between Webtoon continuous scroll and Normal paged reading, with optional page slider and reading percentage controls.

Local Library

Automatically scans your folder to organize your collection. Keeps track of your reading progress and history.

Customizable

Dark/Light themes, metadata editing, and favorites management. Tailor the app to your liking.

High Performance

Built with Electron, local vendor runtimes, and lazy loading for smooth offline reading even on lower-end devices.

Open Source

100% Free and Open Source (MIT License). No ads, no tracking. Built by the community, for the community.

How It Works

KeiYomi is designed to be simple. Just point it to your document folder, and it does the rest.

1. Download

Get the latest installer or portable build from GitHub Releases.

2. Add Files

Place your PDF, EPUB, CBZ, CBR, ZIP, TXT, MD, or DOCX files in the Documents/KeiYomi folder.

3. Read

Open the app, let it scan, and enjoy your reading!
